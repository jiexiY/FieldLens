The environmental method

EMERGE Textbook 1: Data Analysis · Chapter 3, Lesson 3: Vegetation & Water Indices.

NDVI compares near-infrared and red reflectance: (near-infrared − red) / (near-infrared + red). We use this vegetation signal to describe three parts of a mapped campus study line in plain language.

RealLens uses public Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A data from September 22, 2024. This is a single-scene adaptation, not the lesson’s temporal median. The reflectance is scaled using STAC metadata, masked to SCL classes 4, 5, 6, and 7, and reprojected. The source pixels are 10 m; the classification mask is 20 m. Missing pixels remain missing.

Samples every 10 m along each third of the mapped line produce length-weighted means. No descriptive vegetation label is assigned below 50% valid coverage. The bins (below 0.25, 0.25–0.49, 0.50+) are not validated land-cover classes. Historical vegetation does not establish current shade, pavement condition, or safe travel.

The source scene and processing metadata are included in the downloadable grid.

GLOBE Observer and the geoemerge package are not used. The track permits other public environmental data, including Sentinel. This project uses the named EMERGE curriculum method rather than claiming to use every suggested dataset.

Forecasts and published information

NOAA / National Weather Service

NWS public API: hourly rain probability, temperature, wind, and current alerts. Weather is checked when you explicitly prepare or refresh a briefing. The approximately 2.5 km forecast grid cannot reveal individual puddles, local gusts, or slippery surfaces.

UF Campus Closures

Official UF notices and polygons: checked every 15 seconds while the conditions page has a briefing, is visible, and is online. Short caches, source publication, and network delays apply. A geometric overlap is an advisory, not a verified blocked path.

Gainesville RTS

Published rider alerts: checked every 15 seconds on the visible, online bus page, or on request in the voice page. They are not live vehicle positions or ETAs. Publication dates do not prove a notice still applies. Images and embedded content are not interpreted. No background push notifications.

Mapped paths, not navigation

© OpenStreetMap contributors · ODbL supplies the bundled geometry. A shortest connected study line is used to sample environmental context, not to recommend an accessible route. It may contain steps or closures. Building-to-network connections, entrances, crossings, obstacles, and pavement condition are unverified.

The map retrieval timestamp is included in the attributed extract; it is not a field-check date.

Privacy, voice, and prototype limitations

No account, analytics, or location tracking. This browser stores reading preferences, an explicitly saved pair of places and duration, and the RTS filter. The current trip’s places, time, and duration pass between app pages in tab-local session storage; no raw briefing, recording, or transcript is stored there. The weather request sends a campus-area coordinate, not your departure time. Hosting providers may retain standard request logs.

Optional ElevenLabs mode sends a microphone recording of up to 20 seconds through the RealLens server for transcription; reply text is sent for speech generation. RealLens does not persist recordings or transcripts. ElevenLabs may retain them under its policies; its free plan does not provide zero retention. Browser speech may use a remote provider. Microphone access starts only after Talk and permission, with no background listening.

Device screen readers read the controls and descriptions without app audio. Spoken requests receive spoken replies; typed requests stay silent unless you ask for reading aloud. Stop, Escape, leaving the page, or hiding it stops app audio. AI speech powered by ElevenLabs · Provider privacy policy.

Independent, noncommercial research prototype, not an official UF, NASA, NOAA, RTS, or EMERGE service. Not validated with blind or low-vision participants. It does not replace mobility aids, training, official warnings, or observation of actual conditions. Do not modify cane tips or grips based on this app.
